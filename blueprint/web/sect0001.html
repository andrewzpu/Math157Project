1 The Inclusion–Exclusion Principle

1.1 Overview

The goal of this project is to formalize the inclusion–exclusion principle for finite sets in Lean 4 using Mathlib.

Given a finite indexed family of finite sets

\[ \{ S_b\} _{b \in s}, \]

the inclusion–exclusion principle expresses the cardinality of the union

\[ \bigcup _{b \in s} S_b \]

as an alternating sum over intersections of subfamilies.

We formalize this statement using finite sets (‘Finset‘) and prove the result by induction on the indexing set.

The full formal statement is given in Lean as:

1.2 Basic Union Identities

We begin with elementary cardinality results for unions and intersections.

[Two-set union formula] For finite sets \(A,B\),

\[ |A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|. \]

Union.lean, card_union_two

—

[Disjoint union] If \(A\) and \(B\) are disjoint, then

\[ |A \cup B| = |A| + |B|. \]

Union.lean, disjoint_union

—

[Idempotent union] For every finite set \(A\),

\[ |A \cup A| = |A|. \]

Union.lean, same_union

—

1.3 Finite Indexed Unions

We next establish structural lemmas for finite indexed unions.

Let \(s\) be a finite index set and \(b \notin s\). Then

\[ \bigcup _{x \in \{ b\} \cup s} S_x = S_b \cup \bigcup _{x \in s} S_x. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, biUnion_insert

—

\[ \bigcup _{x \in \varnothing } S_x = \varnothing . \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, biUnion_empty

—

For \(x\),

\[ x \in \bigcup _{b \in s} S_b \iff \exists b \in s,\ x \in S_b. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, mem_biUnion

—

1.4 Finite Intersections

We interpret intersections as infima in the lattice structure on ‘Finset‘.

For finite \(T\),

\[ x \in \bigcap _{b \in T} S_b \iff \forall b \in T,\ x \in S_b. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, mem_inf_iff_forall

1.5 Powersets and Alternating Sums

We now study subsets of finite sets and alternating combinatorial sums.

For finite sets \(T,s\),

\[ T \in \mathcal P(s) \iff T \subseteq s. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, mem_powerset

—

If \(s \neq \varnothing \), then

\[ \sum _{T \subseteq s} (-1)^{|T|} = 0. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, alternating_sum_powerset

—

1.6 The Inclusion–Exclusion Principle

We now prove the main theorem.

[Inclusion–Exclusion] Let \(s\) be a finite index set and let

\[ S : \beta \to \mathrm{Finset}(\alpha ) \]

be a finite family of finite sets. Then

\[ \left| \bigcup _{b \in s} S_b \right| = \sum _{\varnothing \neq T \subseteq s} (-1)^{|T|+1} \left| \bigcap _{b \in T} S_b \right|. \]

InclusionExclusion.lean, inclusion_exclusion

—

1.7 Proof Strategy

The proof proceeds by induction on the finite index set \(s\), using:

  • the two-set union formula (1.2),

  • structural lemmas on ‘biUnion‘,

  • and combinatorial cancellation in alternating sums over subsets.
